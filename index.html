Who We Are

About
2oosos

We are 2oosos — a photography project born from the belief that every frame deserves to outlive the moment it was taken. We chase quiet light, raw emotion, and the beauty hiding in the ordinary.

Based in Indonesia, we document life as it unfolds — unposed, unfiltered, and unapologetically real. Every photo we make is a letter written to the future.
